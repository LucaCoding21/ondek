## ONDEK DECKING ADHESIVE

> Ondek Decking Adhesive is specially formulated for fleece and fabric-back vinyl decking membranes. It is an aggressive, solvent based contact adhesive that is suitable for horizontal and vertical surfaces. It exels when applied to exterior-grade plywood, pressure treated lumber, dry, cured concrete and metal flashings.

*(Typos on old site: "exels" → excels, and below "performace" → performance. Flag with owner.)*

## FEATURES
- Compatible with all polyester backed membranes
- Aggressive initial tack at low temperatures
- Outstanding performace on multiple surfaces
- Cures rapidly, forming permanent adhesion
- Bonding resists temperatures between -29ºC to 49ºC (-20ºF to 120ºF)

## TECHNICAL DETAILS
- Can be applied above 10ºC (50ºF)
- Performs best above 15ºC (59ºF)
- Brush, roller or spray application
- Apply to both substrate and Ondek membrane
- Generous 60 minute open time
- Covers 775 square feet of installed vinyl per pail
- 12 month shelf life
- Store between 10ºC (50ºF) and 32ºC (90ºF)
- Does not freeze, remaining liquid down to -5ºC (23ºF)
- Agitate well after warming to 22ºC (72ºF)
