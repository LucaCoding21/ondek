## Ultra Seam page (/ultra-seam/)

Page title: "Ultra Seam - OnDek Vinyl Worx Inc." (built as a blog-style article page)

**ULTRA SEAM**
> Seam technology engineered for stronger welds and long-term waterproof performance
- Buttons: "Request a Quote" · "Download Brochure (PDF)"

### What Makes Ultra Seam Different? (3 checkmark items)
- ✓ **Clean Selvage Edge** — No fleece remnants — no seam contamination
- ✓ **Stops Fleece for Weld** — Backing stops short from the edge of the vinyl
- ✓ **PVC-to-PVC Weld** — Watertight seam welds true "vinyl to vinyl"

### Seam Strength Test
> PVC-to-PVC welded seam tested for strength.
- Video: Vimeo https://player.vimeo.com/video/522076250 (same Ultra Seam test video as home page)

### Comparison: Typical Seam vs Ultra Seam

**Typical Seam**
- ✕ Higher risk of seam contamination
- ✕ Fleece remnants can compromise seam integrity
- ✕ Less confidence in long-term waterproof performance

**Ultra Seam**
- ✓ Clean selvage edge helps prevent seam contamination (no fleece in the weld)
- ✓ Pure vinyl-to-vinyl weld (PVC-to-PVC)
- ✓ In independent testing, the PVC-to-PVC welded seam proved stronger than the vinyl membrane itself

### Ultra Edge Comparison
> A screw-free edge connection designed to stay secure and clean through seasonal movement.

**Typical Edge Clip**
- ✕ May rely on screws for attachment
- ✕ Pulling and detachment can occur with seasonal hot/cold weather
- ✕ Clips can fall off after seasonal expansion and contraction

**Ultra Edge**
- ✓ Positive, screw-free connection (secure mechanical connection)
- ✓ Ultra Clip snaps onto Ultra Flashing for a clean, lasting fit
- ✓ Designed for hot/cold weather to reduce pulling and detachment
